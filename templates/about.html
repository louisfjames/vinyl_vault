Vault Vinyl is an independent online retailer based in Bristol, specialising in curated records, limited-edition pressings, and collector-focused releases. Every album is hand-selected, graded, and photographed in-house to give buyers a clear, honest view of what they’re getting.
We source records from trusted suppliers, independent distributors, and select partners. Every title is inspected before being listed, and we only add albums that meet our standards for quality, authenticity, and pressing accuracy.
Right now, Vault Vinyl stocks brand-new sealed records only. We’re actively working on expanding into pre-owned and collectible used vinyl in the near future, with proper grading and detailed condition notes once that section launches.
